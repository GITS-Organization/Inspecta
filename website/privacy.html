Privacy notice

This notice explains what this website does with data today. It covers the marketing website only, not the Inspecta app itself.

The Inspecta team will add the legal entity, the retention period and a direct contact address before public launch.

What we collect

When you send the Request a Demo or Contact Our Team form, we store only what you type:

  • your full name, work email and company
  • your role (optional) and your message (optional for a demo request)
  • the type of request (demo or contact), the language the site was showing, and the time it was sent

How we use it

We use these details to reply to your request and for nothing else. We do not sell them or share them for advertising.

Where it is stored

They are stored in Cloud Firestore in Inspecta's Firebase project. The security rules let the website create a request only; no visitor can read submitted requests back.

What this website does not do

  • It does not use analytics, advertising cookies or third-party trackers.
  • It saves only your language preference and the time of your last submission (to prevent repeat sends) in your browser's local storage.
  • It does not load fonts or scripts from other sites.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by sending a message through the Contact Our Team form and mentioning the email you used.
